## 冬 Winter
### 小绿 The Greenie

<p style="font-size: 5em; text-align: center; line-height: 1.1; margin-bottom: 30px;">🌲</p>

冬天的晚上，森林里下雪啦。雪花软绵绵的，像棉花糖一样。有一棵小树叫“小绿”，它穿着白色的雪衣裳，安安静静地站着。

One cold winter night, snow was falling softly. It looked like marshmallows! A little tree named Greenie was wearing a white snow coat. He stood very quietly.

突然，小绿觉得痒痒的，“咳咳！”它竟然说话啦！这时候，一位发光的雪精灵飞了出来。

Suddenly, Greenie felt a tickle. "Cough, cough!" Surprise! Greenie could talk! Then, a shiny Snow Fairy appeared.

“别怕别怕，”雪精灵笑着说，“被雪亲吻三次的树，就会得到一份小小的祝福。你现在会说话啦！”

"Don't be scared," the Snow Fairy smiled. "The snow kissed you three times. Now you have a special blessing. You can talk, Greenie!"

雪精灵坐在树枝上说：“祝福要送给别人，才会变得更厉害哦。”小绿看着圆圆的月亮，心里暖暖的。它在想：“我要把祝福送给谁呢？”

The Fairy sat on a branch. "Blessing grows when you share it," she whispered. Greenie looked at the big moon. He felt warm inside. "Who can I share my blessing with?" he wondered.

### 护林员的心愿 The Ranger's Wish

<p style="font-size: 5em; text-align: center; line-height: 1.1; margin-bottom: 30px;">🌟</p>

在森林边的小房子里，住着一个小男孩叫 Ben。Ben 今年有点难过，因为大雪封住了路，没法去买圣诞树。他看着窗外，轻轻叹了一口气：

In a little house nearby, a boy named Ben was sad. Heavy snow blocked the roads, so they couldn't buy a Christmas tree. He looked out the window and sighed,

“唉……没有圣诞树，圣诞老人还会来吗？虽然爸爸保证说，只要是好孩子，有没有树都会有礼物，可是……”这口气飘啊飘，飘到了小绿身边。

"Oh... Will Santa still come if there is no tree?" Dad said good kids always get gifts. But Ben was still worried. His sigh floated through the forest and touched Greenie.

后来，Ben 的爸爸走到了小绿旁边。他也在发愁。虽然他是护林员，但他爱每一棵树，舍不得砍掉它们。突然，小绿说话了：“你在担心 Ben 吗？” 爸爸吓了一跳！小绿说：“把我带回家吧！我可以做 Ben 的圣诞树。等过完节，再把我送回来就好啦。”

Ben's dad walked by Greenie. He was worried. Even though he was a ranger, he loved every tree and didn't want to cut any down. Suddenly, Greenie spoke! "Are you sad for Ben?" Dad was surprised! Greenie said, "Take me home! I can be Ben's Christmas tree. You can bring me back later."

### Ben的家 Ben's Home

<p style="font-size: 5em; text-align: center; line-height: 1.1; margin-bottom: 30px;">🎄</p>

小绿来到了暖暖的房子里。Ben 开心极了，眼睛亮晶晶的！他在小绿身上挂满了纸做的星星和雪花。小绿觉得好开心，心里暖洋洋的。

Greenie came into the warm house. Ben was so happy! His eyes sparkled like stars. He put paper stars and snowflakes on Greenie. Greenie felt very happy and warm inside.

圣诞节晚上了，Ben 睡着了。窗外，雪精灵又来了，她闪闪发光。

It was Christmas night. Ben was asleep. Outside, the Snow Fairy came back. She was glowing.

“小绿，”雪精灵问，“你做了一件好事。现在你可以许一个愿望哦。” 小绿看着 Ben，轻轻地说——**“我希望……所有的小朋友都能感到温暖和快乐。”**

"Greenie," she said, "You did a good thing. Now make a wish!" Greenie looked at Ben and whispered—**"I wish for every child to be warm and happy."**
