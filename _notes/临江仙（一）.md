寄世此身如野草，一番荣色成空。晓霜吹尽小园红。花飞流水外，人立晚烟中。

惟造物者无尽藏，古今长存悠悠。夜阑风静听潮声。小舟归何处，清道照余程。

This body, lodged in time, is grass; one bloom of glory fades to none. Morning frost has swept the garden red. Flowers drift beyond the running stream, I stand within the evening mist.

Only the Lord’s word opens treasure, unchanged by fortune, age, or fall. Across the river night, the tide begins to sound. My little boat knows not its shore; the clear Way lights my remaining road.
