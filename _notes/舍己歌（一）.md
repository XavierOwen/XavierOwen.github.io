幻里寻真真更隐，真从幻处照吾身。

寻到深时无可求，方知舍己一身轻。

In phantoms I sought the truth—

yet truth withdrew, more hidden still.

But from within the phantoms’ veil

its quiet light fell over me.

I searched so deep that nothing beckoned,

no wanting left, no quest to cling.

And only then I came to know:

self laid aside, the soul grows light.

> That Man in the Mirror, is Me.
